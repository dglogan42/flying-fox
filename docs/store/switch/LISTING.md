## Full description

Grow a tiny island home for a clever fox.

Draw tiles from your deck, rotate them into place, and match forest, meadow, water, and rock edges to score. Your den starts neutral — it welcomes every biome. Trigger unique fox abilities as you connect the canopy, complete quests, and chase medals before the deck runs out.

Play Classic runs at your own pace, or take on the Daily island (local seed — no account required).

Designed for TV, tabletop, and handheld. Full controller support.
